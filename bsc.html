Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.)

Course Overview

The Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) is a three-year undergraduate degree that develops scientific knowledge and practical skills.

Duration

3 Years (6 Semesters)

Eligibility

Passed 10+2 with Science stream.

Subjects

  • Physics
  • Chemistry
  • Mathematics
  • Botany
  • Zoology
  • Computer Science

Career Opportunities

Research, Laboratory, Teaching, Higher Studies, Government Jobs.
