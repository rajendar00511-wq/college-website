Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com.)

Course Overview

B.Com. is a three-year undergraduate program focused on accounting, finance, taxation, business management, and economics.

Duration

3 Years (6 Semesters)

Eligibility

Passed 10+2 from a recognized board.

Subjects

  • Financial Accounting
  • Business Law
  • Economics
  • Income Tax
  • Business Statistics
  • Cost Accounting

Career Opportunities

Accountant, Banker, Financial Analyst, CA, CS, MBA.
