Bachelor of Arts (B.A.)

Course Overview

The Bachelor of Arts (B.A.) is a three-year undergraduate degree program that provides students with a strong foundation in humanities and social sciences. It develops communication skills, critical thinking, creativity, and analytical abilities.

Duration

3 Years (6 Semesters)

Eligibility

Passed 10+2 from a recognized board.

Subjects

  • English
  • Hindi
  • History
  • Political Science
  • Economics
  • Sociology

Career Opportunities

Teacher, Civil Services, Journalism, Content Writer, Social Worker, Higher Education.
