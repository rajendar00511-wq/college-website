Master of Arts (M.A.)

Course Overview

The Master of Arts (M.A.) is a two-year postgraduate degree program designed to provide advanced knowledge in humanities and social sciences. The course develops critical thinking, research skills, communication abilities, and subject expertise, preparing students for academic, professional, and competitive careers.

Duration

2 Years (4 Semesters)

Eligibility

A Bachelor's degree (B.A. or equivalent) from a recognized university.

Specializations

  • English
  • Hindi
  • Political Science
  • History
  • Economics
  • Sociology

Career Opportunities

Teaching, Research, Civil Services, Journalism, Content Writing, Public Administration, Social Work, Higher Education, and various Government and Private Sector jobs.
